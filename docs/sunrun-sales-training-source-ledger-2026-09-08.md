# Sunrun sales training and consumer-protection source ledger

Preserved: 2026-09-08

Purpose: preserve the public-source trail used for the SolarComplaint.com research article on Sunrun direct sales training, ethics, consumer protection, and the evolution of its documented sales systems.

## Preservation scope

This file preserves URLs, provenance, dates, and the specific factual proposition each source supports. It is not a mirror or republication of copyrighted training material. Public pages can change or disappear, so the article should continue to distinguish primary corporate/SEC records from authenticated reporting, public onboarding artifacts, and third-party job mirrors.

The research deliberately excludes partner/dealer training as evidence of what Sunrun-employed sales personnel were trained to do. A 2019 Sunrun policy appears inside an SEC-filed channel agreement, but the policy itself expressly applies to Sunrun employees and Marketing and Sales staff; only those employee-facing provisions are used.

## Claim boundary

Do not state that `Power Play 2.0` was in effect in 2025. The public record located as of 2026-09-08 establishes that Sunrun confirmed the authenticity of the manual and that the employee who supplied it said it was current when he left in April 2017. No source located establishes that the manual remained active in 2025, that it was renamed, or that the later `SUNRUN Way Sales Process` descended from it.

### 3. Sunrun Marketing and Advertising Compliance Policy, revised October 10, 2019

URL: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1838987/000119312523141069/d425844dex1019.htm

Location: Exhibit N inside an SEC-filed agreement. The policy begins at the heading `SUNRUN MARKETING AND ADVERTISING COMPLIANCE POLICY (Revised October 10, 2019)`.

Provenance: Sunrun-authored policy reproduced in an SEC filing. Primary for the policy text.

Employee-facing provisions used in the article:
- The policy says Sunrun employees covered by it must comply with the policy, the Code of Business Conduct and Ethics, and applicable state and federal laws.
- It says failure to meet the standards can result in discipline up to termination.
- It says a person who suspects, witnesses, or receives a complaint alleging unethical or illegal practices has a responsibility to report it, with discipline possible for failure to report.
- Claims must be truthful and substantiated, including implied claims.
- Listed examples of prohibited or potentially misleading claims include claims that solar will eliminate a consumer's electric bill, false urgency about expiring government incentives, improper government/utility affiliation, statements that government will pay consumers to go solar, misleading `free` or no-cost claims, specific savings promises, and false urgency.
- The policy states: Marketing and Sales staff are required to comply and Sunrun will provide training on permissible and prohibited claims at least annually.

Direct-sales use: yes, but only the provisions explicitly applying to Sunrun employees and Marketing and Sales staff. Do not use the contractor-specific requirements as proof of employee training.

## Tier D: corroborated job-description mirrors

### 18. `SUNRUN Way Sales Playbook and Toolkit` / `SUNRUN Way Sales Process`

Built In URL: https://builtin.com/job/field-sales-consultant/4373472

Terra.do corroboration: https://www.terra.do/climate-jobs/job-board/Field-Sales-Consultant-Sunrun-8230375/

Provenance: third-party mirrors of a Sunrun job description. The Built In listing was reported as removed March 26, 2025. The page title says `Field Sales Consultant`, while the responsibilities in the body describe senior/regional sales leadership; treat the title/body mismatch as a mirror artifact.

Repeated language in both mirrors supports that the role was expected to:
- partner with Training to create/refine education materials;
- produce the `SUNRUN Way Sales Playbook and Toolkit`;
- deliver new-hire and on-the-job training;
- adapt/refine `Solar Academy`;
- monitor, evaluate and certify competencies;
- drive execution of the `SUNRUN Way Sales Process` through oversight and field visits;
- use QA/QC, scorecards and metrics including close rate, pipeline conversion, self-generated sales, change orders, cancellation rate, customer acquisition cost and NPS;
- communicate and implement business ethics codes.

Limit: no source located proves `SUNRUN Way` was a renamed or direct descendant of `Power Play 2.0`.

## What the public record establishes

1. Sunrun publicly described direct-sales legal/regulatory training as early as 2015.
2. Sunrun said in 2017 that salespeople were trained to use internal guidelines and principles for consultative presentations.
3. Bloomberg authenticated through Sunrun that `Power Play 2.0` was a real Sunrun manual and reported it was described as current through April 2017.
4. A 2019 Sunrun policy explicitly required at least annual training for Marketing and Sales staff on permissible and prohibited claims, including concrete consumer-protection topics.
5. From at least 2021 through 2026, Sunrun repeatedly told shareholders its sales teams were subject to a specific sales code tied to consumer-protection laws.
6. Sunrun's 2025 10-K still described an internally guided consultative sales presentation.
7. Public 2024 onboarding artifacts point to RunX training and new-rep materials, with provenance caveats.
8. Corroborated 2025 job mirrors name a `SUNRUN Way Sales Playbook and Toolkit` and `SUNRUN Way Sales Process`.
9. A 2026 compliance-training job posting describes a new Sales Integrity Program with RunX retraining and complaint-driven accountability controls.

## What remains unknown

- Whether `Power Play 2.0` remained in use after April 2017.
- Whether it was formally retired, renamed, or replaced.
- Whether `SUNRUN Way` incorporated any Power Play language, scripts, objection handling, or behavioral framework.
- The contents and version history of the 2024-2025 RunX sales modules.
- The actual `SUNRUN Way Sales Playbook and Toolkit`.
- Which exact training modules and versions applied to any individual salesperson on a given transaction date.

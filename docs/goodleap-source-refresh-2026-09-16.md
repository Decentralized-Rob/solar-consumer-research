## Government / primary records retained

### Minnesota Attorney General solar-lender action
- Minnesota Attorney General, March 8, 2024: lawsuit against GoodLeap, Sunlight Financial, Solar Mosaic, and Dividend Solar Finance.
- Official release: https://www.ag.state.mn.us/Office/Communications/2024/03/08_SolarLending.asp
- Accuracy boundary: the AG describes $35 million in alleged hidden fees and nearly 5,000 Minnesota solar loans collectively across the four lender defendants. Do not state those totals as GoodLeap-only.
- Federal remand order confirming the claims and parties: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-mnd-0_24-md-03128/pdf/USCOURTS-mnd-0_24-md-03128-1.pdf

### Texas: Lopez v. Meraki Solar et al.
- U.S. District Court, S.D. Texas, No. 7:25-cv-00067, Sept. 26, 2025 memorandum opinion/order.
- Official GovInfo PDF: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCOURTS-txsd-7_25-cv-00067/pdf/USCOURTS-txsd-7_25-cv-00067-0.pdf
- Court record says the homeowners disputed signing or knowing about the GoodLeap loan agreement; GoodLeap moved to compel arbitration.
- Use the court's actual holding, not the allegations alone, when summarizing outcome.

### New Jersey: Shoukat v. GoodLeap / Titan / Sunbeam

## Fresh 2026 court developments to track

These are useful additions to the research queue, but public copy should link to an official court/GovInfo source when available. Secondary docket indexes can be used for discovery only.

### Kane v. GoodLeap — District of Columbia
- D.D.C. No. 22-cv-3777.
- Aug. 12, 2026 memorandum opinion/order reported publicly.
- Issue: contract formation / arbitration in a solar financing dispute involving a signature by the homeowner's adult daughter.
- Upgrade to official court/GovInfo source before treating as a featured public source.

### Arias v. GoodLeap — Maryland
- D. Md. No. 8:25-cv-03122.
- Aug. 11, 2026 memorandum opinion denied GoodLeap's motion to dismiss and compel arbitration.
- Court opinion is available through the federal docket and mirrored by Justia. Prefer the official federal source when added publicly.
- Existing PR treatment correctly frames this as an arbitration/contract-formation ruling, not a merits finding.

### Quayle v. Arizona Solar Solutions / GoodLeap — Texas
- S.D. Texas No. 3:26-cv-00085.
- First amended complaint added GoodLeap June 30, 2026. GoodLeap moved to dismiss and compel arbitration Aug. 5, 2026. Court extended plaintiffs' response deadline to Oct. 5, 2026.
- This is active litigation. No merits finding should be implied.

### GoodLeap v. Solar Cancellation Resource Center / Consumer Advocacy Law Group — Florida
- M.D. Fla. No. 6:26-cv-01072, filed May 14, 2026.
- GoodLeap is the plaintiff. The original complaint was dismissed without prejudice as a shotgun pleading; an amended complaint followed.
- Relevant to the site's consumer-cancellation research, but it is a different category from homeowner claims against GoodLeap.

### Guasp-Santos v. GoodLeap — Florida
- M.D. Fla. No. 8:26-cv-01286.
- Sept. 11, 2026 order accepted the magistrate judge's recommendation granting remand.
- Procedural ruling only. Do not present as a finding on the underlying consumer allegations.

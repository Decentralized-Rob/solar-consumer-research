Featured Florida Sunrun case

Leaking roof. $8,000+ to move the panels. $35,000 to get out.

According to a federal court order summarizing Linda Dow’s amended complaint, she alleged Sunrun would not remove the panels unless she signed a new agreement and paid more than $8,000. When she sought cancellation instead, she alleged Sunrun demanded a $35,000 buyout.

One Sunrun argument was called a “red herring.”

Florida paperwork check

Check what your solar paperwork was supposed to tell you.

Florida Statutes § 520.23 requires a separate solar disclosure covering many items when applicable.

Roof warranties

Florida’s disclosure law calls for a description of roof warranties.

Roof replacement

The disclosure must warn that replacing a roof may require reinstalling the solar system.

System removal fees

One-time and recurring fees must be described, including estimated system-removal fees when applicable.

Liens and UCC filings

The disclosure addresses liens and whether a UCC-1 or fixture filing will be made.

Selling the home

Transfer restrictions and lease-transfer conditions must be disclosed when applicable.

# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Solar Consumer Research is a public-source research project. We expect participation to be respectful, focused on the work, and safe for contributors, visitors, and people discussed in public records.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, accurate, and respectful.
- Discuss ideas, sources, and proposed changes without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and keep personal information out of public GitHub spaces.
- Give and accept good-faith feedback.
- Follow the project’s source-verification and contribution rules.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not allowed:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, hate speech, or discriminatory conduct
- Trolling, insults, personal or political attacks, or repeated bad-faith disruption
- Doxxing or sharing private information without permission
- Posting contracts, contact details, account information, screenshots, or other personal case material
- Unsupported accusations, impersonation, spam, or promotion unrelated to the project
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in moderation

## Scope

This Code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone represents Solar Consumer Research through an official project account or channel.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, reject, or close content that does not meet these standards. Depending on the conduct, they may issue a warning, limit participation, or ban an account from project spaces.

To report a concern privately, email [contact@solarcomplaint.com](mailto:contact@solarcomplaint.com?subject=Code%20of%20Conduct%20report) with the subject **“Code of Conduct report.”** Do not post a report publicly. Reports will be handled as privately as the circumstances allow.
